
# 📊 Grameenphone Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Grameenphone Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful business insights by analyzing revenue, profitability, customer behavior, payment methods, sales channels, and data usage patterns.

The primary objective is to evaluate business performance, identify revenue drivers, understand customer purchasing patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🔍 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Monthly Revenue Analysis
- Evaluates monthly revenue trends.
- Identifies changes in sales performance.
- Compares revenue across different months.

### 2. Revenue by Service Category

Analyzes revenue contributions from:
- Internet Package
- Combo Package
- Postpaid Plan
- Voice Package
- Recharge
- Digital Service

### 3. Revenue by Payment Method

Examines revenue generated through:
- bKash
- Mobile Banking
- Cash
- Nagad
- Card
- Bank

### 4. Revenue by Sales Channels

Evaluates sales performance across different distribution channels.

### 5. Revenue by Customer Segment

Analyzes customer segment contributions:
- Young Professionals
- Family
- Business
- Student
- Premium

### 6. Data Usage by Age Group

Evaluates data consumption patterns across different age groups to understand customer usage behavior.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Revenue Performance:** Total revenue reached 14.11M with a reported profit margin of 42.9%.

2. **Service Category:** Internet Package contributed the highest service-category revenue, accounting for 5.69M (40.31%) of total revenue.

3. **Payment Method:** bKash was the leading payment method, generating approximately 4.3M in revenue.

4. **Customer Segment:** Young Professionals represented the highest revenue-generating customer segment, contributing approximately 4.0M.

5. **Customer Behavior:** The 26–35 age group recorded the highest data usage, with approximately 188K GB.
